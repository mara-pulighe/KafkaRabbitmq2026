# R4 – RabbitMQ Persistence

## Objective

Verify that RabbitMQ can preserve a durable queue and persistent messages across a broker restart.

## Procedure

1. Create a durable queue.
2. Publish persistent messages.
3. Verify that the messages are stored in the queue.
4. Restart the RabbitMQ broker.
5. Inspect the queue and consume the stored messages.
