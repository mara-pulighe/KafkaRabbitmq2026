# K7 – Broker Restart Persistence

## Objective

Verify Kafka's ability to preserve events across broker restarts.

## Procedure

1. Start the Kafka infrastructure.
2. Publish a sequence of events to a topic.
3. Verify that the events are available.
4. Restart the Kafka broker while preserving its stored data.
5. Start the consumer again after the broker restart.
6. Verify that the previously published events are still available and can be consumed without being republished.
