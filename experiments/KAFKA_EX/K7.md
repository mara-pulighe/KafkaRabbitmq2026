# K7 – Broker Restart Persistence

## Objective

Verify Kafka's ability to preserve events across broker restarts.

## Procedure

1. Start the Kafka infrastructure.
2. Publish a sequence of events to a topic.
3. Verify that the events are available.
4. Restart the Kafka broker.
5. Inspect the topic contents after the restart.
6. Consume the stored events.
